The short version

Scrubby Dubby Cleaning collects only what it needs to quote a job, turn up and do the work, and invoice you afterwards. We do not sell your information to anyone.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • The address of the property being cleaned
  • Details about the job itself, including any notes or photos taken for quoting
  • Billing and payment details needed to invoice the work

How we use it

We use your details to quote the job, arrange the booking, carry out the clean, invoice you, and follow up about the work. If you get in touch and we do not end up doing the job, we keep only what is needed for our own records.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or trade your information. We share it only where we have to, for example with an accountant or a payment provider, or where the law requires it.

Photos of our work

We sometimes photograph a finished job for our website or our Facebook page. These photos show the room and the work, not people or personal belongings. If you would rather we did not photograph your property, just say so and we will not.

This website

This site does not run contact forms and does not ask you to create an account. It embeds a Google Map, which is served by Google and subject to Google’s own privacy terms.

Your information

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is wrong. Get in touch and we will sort it.

Getting in touch

Privacy questions can go to scrubbydubbycleaning@gmail.com or 022 609 7587.
